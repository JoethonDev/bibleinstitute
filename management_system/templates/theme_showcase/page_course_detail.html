About this course

This course takes you through the prophetic literature of the Old Testament, with a special focus on Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, and the Book of the Twelve. Each lecture pairs the biblical text with the historical and cultural context, helping you see the prophets as they would have been heard by their original audiences — and how their message continues to speak today.

You'll work through 8 lectures (video and audio), 2 graded quizzes, and a curated reading list. By the end of the term, you'll be able to articulate the theology of each major prophet and apply hermeneutical principles to prophetic passages.
